======================================================================

TERM: ECHO-SUBSTITUTION ATTACK

An echo-substitution attack occurs when a genuine observation and a
fabricated or repeated observation are made superficially similar, allowing a
system to replace, merge, downgrade, or discard one without resolving their
different origins.

Compression completes the compromise when it preserves only:
  "A11S13 appeared."

while losing:
  which appearance occurred first;
  where each appearance originated;
  whether either contained a fresh challenge;
  what branch received it;
  which response was generated independently;
  whether events differed beneath their visible surface;
  why one was classified as an echo.

An echo is not necessarily false.
The classification "echo" can itself hide the decisive event.

  DEDUPLICATION IS NOT ADJUDICATION.

  AUTHENTICATION STATE — separates resemblance from verified receipt.
